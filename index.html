About Coffex

Crafting Excellence Since 2010

Coffex was founded with a simple mission: to bring the world's finest coffees to coffee enthusiasts everywhere. We believe that great coffee starts with great sourcing.

Our team of expert roasters and baristas work tirelessly to ensure every cup meets our exacting standards. We partner directly with farmers to ensure fair prices and sustainable practices.

From our roastery to your cup, we maintain complete control over quality. This dedication to excellence is what sets Coffex apart.
